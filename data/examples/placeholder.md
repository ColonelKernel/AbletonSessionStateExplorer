# Audio placeholders

The demo session references clip audio files such as
`audio/drums_intro.wav`. **No audio is bundled with this repository** — the
paths are placeholders that appear as `audio_file` nodes in the graph and are
counted among the session's uncertain/placeholder elements.

To exercise descriptor extraction, upload your own stems, loops, or a stereo
mixdown in section 2 of the app (WAV, AIFF, FLAC, or MP3 depending on your
installed audio backend) and associate them with a track, clip, or the
project mixdown.
